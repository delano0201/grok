Gork Discord Bot — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Gork Discord bot ("the
Bot") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have regarding it.

1. Information the Bot Collects
- Discord data: your Discord user ID and display name, the channels and
  servers where the Bot is used, and the messages you send to the Bot.
- Chat history: conversation messages are stored locally so the Bot can
  remember context within a conversation.
- Long-term memory: facts about you that you store with /remember, or that
  the Bot distills from your conversations, are kept to personalize future
  replies.
- Server message index: where enabled, recent messages in a server are
  indexed locally to power the /search command.
- Usage metadata: basic counts used to enforce per-user daily limits.

2. Where Data Is Stored
All stored data lives in a local SQLite database on the machine where the
Bot runs (the "host machine"). It is not stored on external servers operated
by the Bot's author.

3. Data Shared with Third Parties
- AI provider: when you chat with the Bot, your messages (and relevant
  context such as conversation history) are sent to the configured
  third-party LLM API provider to generate responses.
- Web lookups: when the Bot performs a live web search or fetches a page you
  asked about, the query or URL is sent to the relevant web service.
- We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to anyone.

4. Data Retention and Deletion
- Conversation context can be cleared at any time with /clear.
- Long-term memories can be deleted with /forget and /forget_all.
- To have your stored data removed entirely, contact the Bot's operator.

5. Children's Privacy
The Bot is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly
collect personal information from children.

6. Changes to This Policy
This Policy may be updated from time to time. Continued use of the Bot after
changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised Policy.

7. Contact
For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact the Bot's operator raceb0i.
